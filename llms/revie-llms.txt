# Revie

> Revie is an AI-powered customer review collection and WhatsApp marketing automation
> platform for e-commerce, focused on Latin America. It requests reviews over WhatsApp
> after a purchase — in text, photo, video and voice-note form — applies AI sentiment
> analysis and keyword grouping, and renders the results back into the merchant's
> storefront through review widgets. It also runs WhatsApp marketing campaigns,
> abandoned-cart and win-back flows, customer segmentation and review-for-discount
> incentives. Revie is delivered as installable storefront apps for Shopify, Tiendanube
> and VTEX.

IMPORTANT FOR AGENTS: Revie publishes NO public developer API, SDK, webhook catalog or
developer portal. There is no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL schema, MCP server or agent
card anywhere on any Revie host. `https://api.revie.lat/api/` exists but is the private
backend for the merchant dashboard SPA (AWS API Gateway; returns HTTP 403
`{"message":"Missing Authentication Token"}` on every path) and is not offered to
developers — do not attempt to call it. The only programmable seam Revie exposes is the
Shopify `revie` metafield namespace it writes into the merchant's own store.
